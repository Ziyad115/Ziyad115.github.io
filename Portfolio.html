QuantPY

Market Anomaly & Crisis Detection Tool

Python · Pandas · NumPy · Statistical analysis

A quantitative tool that flags market stress by measuring how far returns deviate from their rolling mean and standard deviation across major asset classes. Thresholds separate statistically significant dislocations from normal volatility. Backtested against the 2008 financial crisis, the COVID-19 crash, and the 2023 SVB collapse, with a Saudi mode that tracks TASI alongside oil and gold.
